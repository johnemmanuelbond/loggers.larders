###### *Male, Devil*
###### Location(s): [[Thorngarten]], Udun

*Zalviger* is a [Cambion](http://dndroll.wikidot.com/creatures:cambion) who has been held here for a long long time. [[Vorandel]] and Vauric occasionally force him into granting knowledge about [[Pan]] in exchange for a reprieve from the [[Orchestrion]].

##### Interacting with Zalviger

Use implicit skill challenge? DC 15, 3 success before 2 failures. On a successful skill challenge the party gets a great boon, on a failure they get a small boon.

**Low Rolls**
- Zalviger gains leverage:
	- senses that the party needs a quick escape (hears beating at the gates, notices the accumulated wounds)
	- senses John Moore's true nature (gloats at his low standing)
	- feels the eye of Pan upon this place (something important is soon to happen)
	- sees the incoming disaster as his final escape (doesn't need to help the party)

**High Rolls**
- Zalviger looses leverage:
	- The party recognizes that he was bound by these monks, whatever power he has is middling
	- Zalviger has been away from his master for a long time
	- John moore feels a link to zalviger, maybe he can perform this magic himself.


**Small Boons**
	"I.. I can grant you a boon! p-p-p-p-potential! magic powers! robust health! all you need do is destroy that accursed box!"

In exchange for destroying the musoc box, Zalviger offers up...
1. the [[Flaming Falchion]] (Petunia, Gil)
2. a spell slot (Gil, JM, Lemm, Rivka)
3. 60 Temp HP (Petunia, JM, Lemm)

**Great Boons**
	"Wait!! Don't break that circle or you will get NOTHING! No, no I will return in shame, descend in folly, please pllllease give me tiiime I need tiiiiiiiime... I can give *you* time. We just need to make one little deal."

Zalviger can offer one of two deals (depending on the nature of negotation)
1. Zalviger can infuse 5 creatures with instant vigor, granting the benefits of a short rest. In exchange, Zalviger needs blood from each of the five creatures to perform this magic.
2. Zalviger will fight off all intruders, granting the party a short rest. In exchange Zalviger needs to bond with a party member, it involves a blood pact to "Grant me license. grant me direction, grant me a charge so that I *may* charge"
