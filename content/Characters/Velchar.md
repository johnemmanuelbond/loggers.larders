#### Description
Velchar carries himself effortless confidence. He has walnut brown hair; immaculately groomed and gathered behind his shoulders with a bronze clasp. His face is sharp and angular, weathered yet warm. He has deep smile lines. While here he wears the austere blue robes and hempen belt of Thorngarten, his manicured fingers betray someone who cares about his appearance. He is handsome in an undeniably cultivated way, quick with a warm smile, lingering eye contact, and the sort of easy charm that seems equally practiced and genuine. Only when that smile broadens does its unsettling edge reveal itself: each tooth has been meticulously filed into a delicate point, lending his laughter the faintest suggestion of a predator's grin. Draped in refinement, he has the air of a scoundrel, and this contradiction suits him.
