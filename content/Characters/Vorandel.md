###### *Male, [[Northern Elves]], ancient*
###### Location(s): [[Thorngarten]]

#### Description
Vorandel is difficult to place at a glance. His long silver hair falls in immaculate sheets from a pronounced widow's peak, framing a face that seems untouched by age until one notices the countless hairline creases etched across his skin. The wrinkles are so impossibly fine that, rather than making him appear old, they catch the light like the grain of polished ivory, lending him an almost luminous youthfulness. His pale eyes are patient and unwavering, and when he speaks he gives the impression that every word has been weighed over centuries before being allowed to leave his lips.

#### The High Abbott
There is nothing theatrical about the reverence Vorandel inspires. Monks quiet themselves when he enters and resolve replaces the grief their eyes. He moves slowly, with measured purpose, and embodies the very patience and respect he expects from his followers.

Vorandel was a young elf when [[Adhael Anar, Caendaur Fuirith]] ended the [[War of Salt and Snow]] in an elvish victory. As a result, Vorandel's very father was spared from the duty of a suicidal mission. Vorandel has since dedicated himself to Adhael's memory, and charged himself with guarding the last sample of his blood. Vorandel never had the honor of meeting Adhael before his downfall, but has been dutifully gathering like-minded followers to mournfully protect the seat of his power: Shimmer grove.
