*Sant Eigenost*, in elvish. Thorngarten is an ancient monstary commnity of elvish aesthetics. They revere the memory of [[Adhael Anar, Caendaur Fuirith]], the warden of the floes who won the elves the [[War of Salt and Snow]].

![[thorngarten.png]]

*Gleaming grey stone foundations rise from the slopes of a jagged rocky hill so that the structure atop it may sit on a flat smooth surface surrounded by patios and gardens. The main building of Thorngarten is a lodge made of the same grey stone and beams dark black wood. The roofline, curved like an bison's horn, is made of teal stone shingles. The main doors are framed by a maroon curtain and open to a patio with a planted ash tree whose leaves are have begun to turn orange. Connected with a small covered walkway, a separate multi-tiered pagoda sits on another stone structure to the east and downhill of the main lodge.*

## Locations
Thorngarten consists of two building set atop a rocky outcropping. Surrounding the outcropping, is a ring of petrified [[Skinsplinterer]]'s which are part of a powerful magical ward which deters powerful magical creatures from entering. The monstary has three parts: [[Thorngarten#The Lodge|The Lodge]], [[Thorngarten#The Eastern Solar|The Eastern Solar]], and [[Thorngarten#The Tunnels|The Tunnels]] which connect them:
![[thorngarten-grounds.png]]

### The Lodge
A two-story wooden and stone building the Lodge houses all of the monks and is where they do their typical day-to-day chores. The Lodge is built around a central dining and sermon hall where the monks eat and discuss their business.

Surrounding this central hall, spread across two levels, are all the monks' personal quarters, kitchens, store rooms, and a prayer room. The lodge has three entrances, one set of curtains which open to the ash tree on the patio, and two sets of double doors: one to the prayer room and one to the kitchen.

The south side of the lodge rises up an extra floor to a stained glass window. Depicted in the window is a motif of a gleaming eye whose iris is filled with a two-pronged tower.

### The Tunnels
![[thorngarten-basement.png]]

Spread over three levels, Thorngarten boasts a sprawl of tunnels which connect The Lodge to The Eastern Solar. The walls here consist of dark stonework and lines of a charcoal-colored mortar. The monotany is only occasionally broken up by tapestries and iron braziers.

The first level holds several kegs of [[Communion Wine]] below the prayer room, as well as some bedchambers below the kitchens. 

The second level holds a small storage and workroom, and the ritual chambers.

The third level holds the Thorngarten crypt, and below that is the vault.

#### Workroom

A broad worktable dominates the center of the room where two monks quietly sort, catalog, and repair various bronze fittings, candles, and ritual implements. Several stout chests line the walls beside shelves stocked with rope, tools, replacement tiles, lamp oil, and other practical supplies used to maintain the monastery. The only sounds are the occasional scratch of a quill and the soft clink of metal against stone.

**Mid rolls**

**High Rolls**

## Heist
The Thorngarten monks are there explicitly to protect artifacts related to [[Adhael Anar, Caendaur Fuirith|Adhael]] and his memory. The party wants one of these artifacts, [[The Sample]], and is explicitly moving against the monks' interests. Therefore, the party must navigate the monastery as if it is hostile.

The party makes a group check upon trying to gain entry (stealth / deception / persuasion). If more than half of them meet each threshold, certain effects happen:
- 12 - The party gains entry, but raise 1 ***suspicion*** level
- 17 - The party gains entry

##### Suspicion
***Suspicion***: Measured on a scale of 1-6. It starts at 1. The following things increase suspicion
- Caught by a monk while wandering around
- Casting a spell with verbal/somatic components in clear sight
- Being seen with a visible weapon
- Indiscretely asking around about adhael

Based on ***suspicion***, the players encounter a number of monks as they enter a location if they roll a 1 on the patrol die:

If they players encounter a monk, they get one round of actions to do something (talk, hide, run, fight) with a DC based on the suspicion table. A failure will result in the party getting kicked out of Thorngarten.

*Notes*
- The glass case within the parchment is clearly enchanted. Detect magic reveals evocation and abjuration. Opening/breaking the case requires a DC 14 constitution save or become paralyzed for an hour. 
- *Declaration*: A long piece of vellum, perfectly preserved,  written in elvish in an elegant hand. The author rather verbosely declares 'The Floes' as his own personal fief. It is signed "Adhael Anar, Caendar Fuirith" signs . An extremely high check recognizes the handwriting and tone as having similarities to the journal you've read from.
- *Tapestry*: An 8 foot long tapestry depicting the fall of Adhael. There are scenes of a wizard discovering a shimmering tree (reminds you of old gran mimsdottir's quilt). That same wizard leading armies, then holding court, before eventually being laid low by a cadre of glowing elves. At their head is an armored woman with long pitch black hair and grey eyes. Her followers bear the marks of Griffin, Swan, and Mole. The swan is recognizable as the symbol of the [[Neth'Tel]].
