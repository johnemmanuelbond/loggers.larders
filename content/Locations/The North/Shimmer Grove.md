#### Entering Shimmer Grove:
Following the steady southward trail of this stream leads you out and around the final twisting turn of this gulley. The tall mountainous walls on either side begin to fall back and a true wide valley unfolds before you.

Dark stone faces dark with runoff from yesterdays storm greet you from the south, west, and east. At this moment you realize the path you've taken is perhaps the only way in or out of this place as the mountain ridges on every other side are taller, starker, and more foreboding than the ground you've already traversed.

Carrying the scent of wet stone, pine resin, and clean fresh water, the breeze in this valley gently rustles the leaves and needles of the forest spilling down the mountainside. Deep green waves of pine and fir, broken up here and there by the warm colors of this early autumn. Even now a few of the trees have started to change into the rich patches of amber, copper and crimson that you know will soon engulf these slopes.

While you indeed sit in the lowliest of the surrounding mounts, you still look down upon the valley floor, swallowed beneath layers of canopy and drifting mist. Occasional clearings in the treeline dot the landscape, containing  rocky outcroppings of stone pushing through earth or beneficent meadows of underbrush swaying in the wind.

Looking out at this vista your mind is drawn back to that faint sheen on the air, like sunlight reflecting off of rippling waves. Your mind is brought to the gleaming white tree you saw in the astrolabe, that glowing haze in the southern sky, and the pinpoint of light emanating from Val pointing you south.

# Adhael's Ritual

I'd suggest making the ritual's description increasingly beautiful and increasingly wrong. The players should feel as though they are performing something sacred before they realize that the ritual is using them.

> “The procedure is simple, but it will require conviction. Place the pyramid upon my body as you light its pyre. Do not allow the smoke to harm the tree. It must survive at all costs.”

## The Ritual Begins

_The pyramid is placed upon Kargoe's ruined body._

_For a moment, nothing happens._

_Then the first flame catches._

_It is not a bright flame, nor does it burn with the familiar colors of fire. It is a pale, colorless thing that clings to the pyramid's surface and crawls slowly across the dead man's armor. The metal begins to hum. Beneath it, the vine embedded in Kargoe's flesh contracts, its red thorns pressing deeper into the withered skin._

_The tree responds._

_Every silver-blue leaf shivers at once._

_The sound is delicate, almost musical, but it travels across the grove with impossible clarity. The stream stills. The spirits turn their faces toward the hill. Even the strands restraining Zalviger seem to pause, suspended in the air like threads caught in an unseen current._

_Smoke begins to pour from the pyramid._

_It does not rise. It spills outward, flowing over the earth in heavy, dark ribbons. It gathers around Kargoe's body, obscuring his limbs, his armor, and the roots that hold him in place._

_Then something within the smoke moves._

_At first, it resembles a shadow cast by a person standing just beyond the fire. It lengthens, bends, and rises. A second outline appears within it, then a third, each one struggling to occupy the same space. The smoke pulls upward in a sudden, violent column, and the greater shape of Adhael emerges from within._

_He is enormous._

_Not in stature, but in presence. His form stretches above the hill like a man remembered by something that never truly understood the shape of a man. His robes trail into smoke. His hands are too long. His face is almost recognizable, though it shifts whenever you try to focus upon it._

_The spirits cry out._

_Some recoil. Others kneel. A few reach toward him with expressions of desperate recognition._

_Adhael does not look at them._

_His attention is fixed upon you._

> “Now.”

_The word does not sound loud, but it enters the mind with the weight of a command._

_The smoke surges outward._

_Five currents begin to form, each pulled toward a different member of the party. They twist around your bodies without touching you, responding not to movement but to thought. The ritual is waiting._

_It is waiting for you to decide what you want._

_Your memories rise first. Not as images, but as sensations: the weight of a weapon, the warmth of a hand, the taste of blood, the sound of a voice you had nearly forgotten. The ritual reaches into these things and finds the shape of your desire._
