*An ornate gold music box with serene faces embossed on the sides.*

As an action you can activate the box. For 1 minute all creatures within 60ft that can hear the music box have advantage on wisdom saving throws, but disadvantage on attack rolls.

Additionally, any fiends which hear this music have disadvantage on all checks, saves, and attack rolls and must make a DC 15 wisdom saving throw each time they take an action or spend their turn weeping.
