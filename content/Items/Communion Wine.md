*A small bottle of dark red liquid that smells of tannins and cherry*

Drinking this wine restores 4d4+4 hit points.

Additionally, the creature must make a DC 10 constitution saving through or become poisoned (drunk)

Finally, if the user is _[[Adhael Anar, Caendaur Fuirith#Blood Plague|infected]]_, they gain the effects of a _[See Invisiblity](https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/spell:see-invisibility)_ spell.
