_A cyan leaf with a vein of red running down the middle. It sees to shimmer and ripple like water made of light._

This object can be used as a component for magical spells and rituals. It can substitute for up to 100 gold worth of components for divination, conjuration, or necromancy spells.

Additionally, if eaten it grants the user the effects of a *Protection from Evil and Good* spell for 8 hours.
