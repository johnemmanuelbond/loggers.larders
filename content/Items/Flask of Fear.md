*A round glass flask with swirling dark motes of dust.*

Make an attack roll against a target within 30ft. The target and each creature in a 30ft cone behind it must succeed on a DC 14 wisdom saving throw or be frightened for a minute.

While frightened, a creature must take the Dash action to flee combat by the safest available route on each of its turns, unless there is nowhere to move. At the end of each of its turns the creature can make a wisdom save to end the effect.
