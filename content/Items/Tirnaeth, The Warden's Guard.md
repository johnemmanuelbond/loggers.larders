*A shining blue steel longsword engraved with runic wings. The blade edge tapers gravefully to a point.*

Multiple creatures may attune to Tirnaeth. A creature can attune to this weapon with an action by using the blade to draw blood.

Attuned creatures are immune to possession and any other effect which would disconnect their spirit from their body.

Attuned creatures have a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this weapon.

When a creature takes damage from an attack made with Tirnaeth, it must make a DC 17 Charisma saving throw or have its speed reduced to 0 for 1 round. A creature which succeeds is then immune for 24 hours.

A creature holding Tirnaeth may cast *Dispel Evil and Good* (DC 17) without expending a spell slot or material components. The DC Once this spell is cast, it cannot be cast again using Tirnaeth for 24 hours.

An attuned creature holding Tirnaeth can use a bonus action to teleport the sword to another attuned creature. The sword appears in the creature's hand if one is available, or at their feet elsewise.
