*An oily black necklace set with a ring of magenta gems.*

While attuned to this necklace, you have darkvision out to 60 ft. 

Additionally, the you can attempt to throw shade at one target that can hear 
you within 60 ft.

Make an intimidation or insight check against a target (contested by the target's opposite skill). On a success you learn the target's greatest insecurity and have advantage on all Wisdom and Charisma checks and saves against their effects. Additionally, the target takes an extra 1d12 psychic damage the first time you hit them with an attack on your turn. On a failure, you suffer these effects against the target.
